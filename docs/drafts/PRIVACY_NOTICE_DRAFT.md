**Jacquot Digital Solutions ("JDS"), Walkerton, Ontario — Privacy Notice (Beta)**

**Who we are.** JDS provides online ordering for independent cafés. Each café ("the Shop") decides what it
sells; JDS runs the software on the Shop's behalf. Privacy contact: `<PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL>`.

**What we collect and why**
- *Customers:* name, email, phone (optional), order details and pickup time — to place your order, tell the
  Shop, and send order updates. Loyalty data if the Shop uses loyalty.
- *Shop staff/owners:* name, email, role, sign-in activity — to run accounts and keep them secure.
- *Technical:* IP address, device/browser info, error logs — for security and fixing problems.
We do not sell personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising.

**Payment.** During the beta, orders are paid at pickup. We do not collect card numbers.

**Sharing.** With the Shop you order from; with service providers that host data or send email for us
(`<HOSTING_PROVIDER>`, `<DATABASE/STORAGE_PROVIDER>`, `<EMAIL_PROVIDER>`), under contract; or when required by law.
Some providers may store data outside Canada (`<LIST COUNTRIES>`), where it may be accessible to local authorities.

**Retention.** Order records are kept while the Shop's account is active and up to `<N>` months after, then
deleted or anonymized. Encrypted backups are kept up to 14 days.

**Your rights.** You may ask to access or correct your information, or withdraw consent (which may mean we can't
take your order). Contact `<PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL>`. You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy
Commissioner of Canada.

**Safeguards.** Encrypted connections, access limited by role, separated data per Shop, encrypted backups.
We will notify affected people and the Privacy Commissioner of breaches that pose a real risk of significant harm.

**Changes.** We'll post updates here with a new date. Last updated: `<DATE>`.

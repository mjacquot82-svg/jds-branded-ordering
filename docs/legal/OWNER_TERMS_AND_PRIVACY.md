## Part A – Terms of Service

### 1. What this is
JDS Branded Ordering ("the Service") lets your café run its own branded ordering app. Your customers can order ahead, pay, and pick up. "We" or "JDS" means Jacquot Digital Solutions, operating as JDS Studio. "You" means the café business that signs up, and anyone you let use your account.

### 2. It's a beta
The Service is in a private beta. Features may change, and there may be bugs or downtime. Please don't rely on it as your only way to take orders. Report problems to contact@jdsstudio.ca or with the in-app "Report a problem" link.

### 3. Price
The beta starts in November 2026. The Service is free during the beta, and JDS takes **0% of your sales**. We'll give you at least 30 days' notice by email before any charge. You decide whether to continue, and nothing is charged automatically.

### 4. Payments
- Customer payments go through **your own Square or Clover account**. You can also choose to accept payment at pickup, which is off by default.
- Payment is between you, your customer, and your payment provider. **JDS never sees or stores card numbers.** Card details are entered on Square's or Clover's own secure page.
- Your payment provider's fees and terms apply. Refunds, chargebacks, and disputes are handled by you through your provider.
- When you connect Square or Clover, we store an access token, encrypted, so the Service can create checkouts and confirm payments. You can disconnect at any time.

### 5. Your account
Keep your logins private, including staff PINs, and tell us right away if you think someone else has used them. You're responsible for activity under your account and staff logins.

### 6. Your responsibilities
- Keep your menu, prices, taxes, allergen information, and hours accurate.
- Fulfil the orders you accept, and handle customer service and refunds.
- Follow the laws that apply to your shop, including consumer protection, food safety, and anti-spam (CASL) laws if you send marketing messages.
- Show your customers the in-app privacy notice, which the app does by default, and only use customer data to run your shop.

### 7. Your content and data
You own your menu, photos, logo and branding, and your customer and order data. You give JDS permission to host, copy, and display it only as needed to run and support the Service. Starter images that JDS supplies may be used only inside the Service.

### 8. Acceptable use
Don't use the Service for anything illegal, to sell restricted items, to send spam, or to try to break or overload it. We may suspend an account that does.

### 9. Feedback
If you send ideas or feedback, JDS may use them to improve the product without owing you anything.

### 10. No warranty, and limits on liability
The Service is provided "as is" during the beta. To the extent the law allows, JDS is not liable for lost sales, profits, or data, or for indirect damages. JDS's total liability is limited to the greater of (a) the fees you paid JDS in the 12 months before the claim, or (b) CAD $100. Nothing here limits rights that can't be limited by law.

### 11. Ending
Either of us can end this at any time by email. When it ends, you can ask for an export of your data within 30 days. After that, we delete it as described in Part B.

### 12. Changes

## Part B – Privacy Policy (for café owners and staff)

JDS follows Canada's *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act* (PIPEDA).

### 1. Who is responsible
- **For your own account information,** JDS is responsible.
- **For your customers' information,** your café is responsible, and JDS processes it **on your behalf** as your service provider. We use it only to run your ordering app, never to sell, rent, or market to your customers ourselves.
- **Privacy contact:** Marc Jacquot, contact@jdsstudio.ca.

### 2. What we collect about you
- **Account details:** name, business name, email, phone, address, password (stored hashed by our sign-in provider), and staff names and PINs.
- **Shop setup:** menu, prices, hours, branding, tax settings, and payment-provider connection status, with tokens stored encrypted.
- **Usage and technical data:** sign-in times, error logs, and basic device and browser information, used to keep the Service secure and working.

### 3. Why we collect it
To create and secure your account, run your app, send service emails such as sign-up confirmations, provide support, prevent fraud, and improve the Service. We ask for your consent when you sign up. We only use your information for these purposes unless we ask you again.

### 4. Who we share it with (service providers)
| Provider | What for | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Canner | App and database hosting | Canada |
| Supabase | Sign-in / authentication | Canada (ca-central-1) |
| Resend | Sending sign-up confirmation and receipt emails | United States (Resend stores email content, logs, and account data in the US) |
| Square / Clover | Payments, through your own account | Under their own privacy policies |

Resend, and possibly Square or Clover, process data outside Canada, mainly in the United States, where it may be accessible to local authorities under local law. We don't sell personal information.

### 5. How long we keep it
- While your account is active.
- After you leave, we delete or anonymize it within 90 days, except where the law requires us to keep records longer.
- Backups expire within 14 days.

### 6. How we protect it
We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted payment tokens, hashed passwords, access limited to people who need it, and separation between shops so one shop can't see another's data. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we'll notify you and the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as PIPEDA requires.

### 7. Your rights
You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information, or withdraw consent, by emailing contact@jdsstudio.ca. We'll answer within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).

### 8. Customer requests
If one of your customers asks us about their data, we'll pass the request to you and help you respond.

# Privacy Notice – {SHOP_NAME} Ordering App

**Last updated:** October 30, 2026

This ordering app is run by **{SHOP_NAME}** ("the café"). The café is responsible for your personal information. The app is provided by **Jacquot Digital Solutions, operating as JDS Studio** (Walkerton, Ontario, Canada), which handles your information only on the café's behalf to run this app.

## What we collect
- **Your contact details:** name, email, and phone number, so the café can identify your order and reach you about it.
- **Your orders:** items, notes, pickup time, order history, and loyalty points, if the café offers them.
- **Your account,** if you create one: email and password. The password is stored securely by our sign-in provider, and the café can't see it.
- **Basic technical data:** device and browser information and error logs, used to keep the app secure and working.

**We never see or store your card number.** When you pay online, you enter your card on the café's payment provider's secure page (Square or Clover). We only receive whether the payment succeeded.

## Why we use it
- To take, prepare, and hand over your order.
- To send your receipt and order emails.
- To show your past orders and loyalty points.
- To keep the app secure and prevent fraud.

The café won't send you marketing messages unless you agree to them, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Your information is never sold.

## Who it's shared with
Your information is shared only with companies that help run the app:
- Canner for hosting, in Canada
- Supabase for sign-in, in Canada
- Resend for emails, in the United States
- Square or Clover for payments

Because Resend, and possibly the payment provider, process data in the United States, your information may be accessible to authorities there under US law.

## How long it's kept
Order records are kept for as long as the café needs them for business and tax records, usually up to 7 years for financial records. If you delete your account, your profile is removed within 30 days, and remaining order records are kept only as the law requires. Backups expire within 14 days.

## How it's protected
The app uses encrypted connections, secure sign-in, and access limited to the café's authorized staff. Each café's data is kept separate.

## Your choices and rights
You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by contacting the café at **{SHOP_CONTACT_EMAIL}**. You can also email JDS Studio at contact@jdsstudio.ca, and we'll pass your request to the café. You'll get an answer within 30 days. If you have concerns, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

By placing an order, you agree to your information being used as described here.
